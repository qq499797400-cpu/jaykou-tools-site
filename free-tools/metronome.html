A practice metronome that keeps perfect time

Rock-solid timing

Every click is scheduled ahead on the audio hardware clock, so it never drifts, even when the page is busy. Measured in a 60-second render, clicks land on the exact sample.

Any meter and accent

1/4 to 12/8 or any custom meter up to 16 beats, with each beat set to accent, normal, soft or silent. Compound meters such as 6/8 and 12/8 come grouped in threes.

Subdivisions, swing and polyrhythms

Hear eighths, triplets, sixteenths, quintuplets or swing eighths, and layer a second pulse such as 3 against 2 or 4 against 3 with its own sound and volume.

节奏训练器

Raise (or lower) the tempo by a few BPM every few bars until you reach your target, hands-free.

寂静的酒吧

Play a few bars with clicks, then a few without. If you are still on the beat when the clicks come back, your time is solid.

Made for the stage and the practice room

Stage mode shows a huge tempo and beat display, the screen can flash or your phone vibrate on each beat, and presets and a practice log are saved on your device.

Practice tips

Start slow

Play a passage at a tempo where it is clean, then let the tempo trainer add a few BPM at a time.

Feel the subdivisions

Turn on eighths or sixteenths for fast passages: the in-between clicks keep your notes even.

Put the click on 2 and 4

For swing and pop, set beats 1 and 3 to silent and accent 2 and 4, like a drummer's backbeat.

Use headphones for recording

The downloaded click track lines up exactly with your project's grid when you set the same tempo.

节拍器常见问题

这个节拍器是免费的吗？

是的。无需账户即可试用，也无需安装任何东西。

时间安排有多准确？

点击是根据您设备的音频时钟安排的，而不是页面计时器，因此当页面繁忙时，它们不会漂移或卡顿。在我们的测试中，每次 60 秒渲染的点击都落在理想节拍的 1 毫秒内。

What do the beat circles mean?

Each circle is a beat of the bar. Tap one to switch it between accent (orange), normal, soft (dashed) and silent (dotted).

How does the tempo for 6/8 work?

The tempo counts the lower number of the time signature: in 6/8 it is eighth notes per minute. For a feel of two dotted-quarter beats at 60, set 180.

How do I play a polyrhythm?

Choose a time signature for the main pulse, then pick the other pulse under Polyrhythm. In 2/4 with 3, you hear 3 against 2; in 3/4 with 4, 4 against 3.

Does it keep playing in the background or with the screen off?

It keeps playing in a background tab in most desktop browsers. Phones may pause web audio when the screen locks; the page keeps the screen on while the metronome runs where the browser allows it.

Can I use it in my recording software?

Yes. Download a click track as a WAV file with your current settings and drop it into your project at the same tempo.

Is the voice count included?

Not yet. The sounds are synthesised in your browser (click, wood block, beep, cowbell, hi-hat and electronic); there are no recorded samples.
